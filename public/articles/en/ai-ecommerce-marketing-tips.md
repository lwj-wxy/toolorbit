### The Pain Points of Listing WritingFor many cross-border sellers, writing persuasive Product Titles and Bullet Points in a non-native language is a huge challenge. Traditional machine translation is often stiff and fails to meet search algorithms' needs.

### 5 Tips for Optimizing with AI<ol>* **Natural Keyword Embedding:** Avoid keyword stuffing. Use AI to analyze hot terms and integrate them naturally into descriptions.
* **Emotional Expression:** Consumers don't buy features; they buy solutions. AI helps convert 'This lamp is bright' to 'Comfortable light for your late-night work sessions.'
* **Multi-language Localization:** For different regions (like LatAm or SE Asia), AI can adjust the tone to better fit local cultural habits.
* **Shorten Complex Sentences:** Mobile shopping is dominant. Concise, powerful copy is more effective on small screens.
* **A/B Testing:** Quickly generate two versions of copy and fine-tune based on conversion data.
