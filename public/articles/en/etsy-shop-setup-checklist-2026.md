# 2026 Etsy Shop Setup Checklist: How to Open a Shop Without Getting Suspended

Opening a new Etsy shop in 2026 gives you instant access to over 90 million active global buyers. However, Etsy has tightened its identity verification and automated anti-fraud security filters. 

Many new sellers find their shop suspended within minutes of creation simply because their bank registration name, IP region, tax ID, or credit card details triggered automated risk flags.

This checklist provides a step-by-step guide to opening a verified, suspension-free Etsy shop in 2026. Before listing your initial products, model your fee expectations using the [Etsy Fee Calculator](/tools/ecommerce/etsy-fee-calculator).

---

## 1. Pre-Registration Verification Checklist

Before visiting Etsy.com to register, gather the following verified documents. **All information must match 100% across all documents.**

```text
+------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| REQUIRED DOCUMENT                  | VERIFICATION RULE                                        |
+------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Government-Issued Photo ID         | Passport or Driver's License (Unexpired, high-res scan)  |
| Primary Bank Account               | Must be in your home country; name matches ID exactly    |
| Credit / Debit Card                | Visa/Mastercard (Must support international USD billing) |
| Tax Identification Number          | SSN / EIN (US), UTR (UK), or National Tax ID             |
| Residential Address Proof          | Utility bill or bank statement showing legal address     |
+------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
```

> **Critical Warning**: Never use a VPN or proxy server during registration. Registering from an IP address that does not match your residential address or bank country is the #1 cause of immediate automated suspensions.

---

## 2. The 5-Step Registration Process

### Step 1: Set Up Your Shop Preferences
Select your home country, shop currency, and language. 
* **Currency Rule**: Always select the currency of your bank account. If you operate from the UK with a GBP bank account, select **GBP (£)** as your shop currency. Choosing USD will incur a permanent **2.5% currency conversion fee** on all sales.

### Step 2: Choose Your Shop Name
Your shop name must be 4–20 characters, with no spaces or special characters.
* Check availability before registering. If your target name is taken, append keywords such as `Studio`, `Design`, `Co`, or `Crafts`.

### Step 3: Stock Your First Listing
Etsy requires you to create at least 1 listing during setup ($0.20 listing fee applies).
* Upload at least 5 clear, high-resolution product photos.
* Fill out the title and 13 tags using long-tail phrases. Check keywords using the [Ecommerce Keyword Analyzer](/tools/ai/keyword-analyzer).

### Step 4: Set Up Payment Account (Etsy Payments)
Connect your bank account to receive payouts.
* Enter your routing number, account number, and billing address.
* Upload a crisp color scan of your government ID if prompted. Ensure no glare or truncated corners.

### Step 5: Set Up Billing Card & Enable 2FA
Add a credit or debit card for shop fee billing, and set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via an authenticator app.

## 3. How to Avoid New Shop Suspensions (Anti-Fraud Tips)

Etsy's fraud detection bots automatically flag new accounts that display suspicious behavior. Follow these rules during your first 90 days:

```text
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| NEW SHOP ANTI-SUSPENSION RULES                                                                        |
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| DO NOT                             | WHY IT TRIGGERS A SUSPENSION                                     |
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Use a VPN or Proxy                 | IP mismatch flags security bots for account takeover risk        |
| List Trademarked / Brand Items     | IP infringement bots instantly flag Disney, Nike, Gucci, etc.    |
| Change Bank Accounts Immediately   | Changing payout details right after opening triggers a fraud lock |
| Copy Competitor Photos             | Reverse image bots detect stolen main photos                     |
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 4. First 30 Days Action Plan for Success

1. **Complete Your Shop Announcement & About Page**: Add a shop banner, owner bio, and story photos. Shops with complete About sections receive higher buyer trust scores.
2. **Publish at Least 10 to 15 Listings**: Single-listing shops struggle to rank. Building a catalog of 15+ listings gives Etsy's algorithm more data points to index your store.
3. **Set Up Shipping Profiles**: Build clear shipping profiles with accurate handling times and tracking carrier details.
