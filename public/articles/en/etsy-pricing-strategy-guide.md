# Etsy Pricing Strategy: Build a Price That Can Support the Business

The right Etsy price covers more than materials. It needs to leave room for platform fees, packaging, shipping, labor, mistakes, and the time required to run the shop.

Use the [Etsy Pricing Calculator](/tools/ecommerce/etsy-pricing-calculator) to test the arithmetic, then compare the result with similar products and the value your listing provides.

## Find the minimum sustainable price

Write down the cost of one order before looking at competitor prices:

- materials or production cost;
- packaging and inserts;
- shipping label and supplies;
- direct labor;
- listing and payment fixed fees;
- percentage-based Etsy fees;
- a small allowance for refunds, replacements, and damaged orders.

This is your cost floor. A competitor's price is not evidence that the same price works for your shop. They may buy materials in larger quantities, use a different production process, or accept a lower margin.

## Use a target profit, not a markup guess

Decide how much contribution profit one order should produce. Then calculate backward:

```text
Required order revenue =
(non-percentage costs + target profit + fixed fees)
/ (1 - percentage fee rate)
```

Run the formula with and without an Offsite Ads fee. If the price only works when advertising is absent, you have found a risk in the model rather than a reason to ignore the fee.

## Decide how to handle shipping

Free shipping and buyer-paid shipping are both valid. The important question is whether the total amount shown to the buyer is competitive and profitable.

For free shipping, include the expected shipping cost in the item price and explain the delivery promise clearly. For buyer-paid shipping, use accurate shipping profiles and check that the shipping charge does not make the checkout total look unreasonable.

Test both versions in the calculator. The better option is the one that produces a healthy contribution margin without weakening conversion.

## Compare the market without copying it

Review the first page of comparable Etsy listings and record:

- total price including shipping;
- number and quality of photos;
- personalization or variation options;
- delivery time;
- review themes and repeated complaints;
- what the description promises.

Price is only one part of the offer. A clear size guide, better photos, faster dispatch, or a more useful bundle can justify a higher price when the listing explains the difference.

## Price variations and bundles deliberately

Variations should have a visible reason for their price difference: size, quantity, material, license, or customization. Keep the steps easy to understand and verify that every variation still covers its own cost.

For bundles, calculate the cost and labor of the complete bundle first. A discount is useful when it increases average order value or reduces handling time. It is not useful when it turns a profitable single item into an unprofitable bundle.

## Review price performance monthly

Track more than revenue:

- conversion rate;
- average order value;
- contribution profit per order;
- refund and replacement rate;
- ad-attributed order share;
- time spent producing and supporting each order.

If traffic is strong but profit is weak, raise the price or reduce the cost. If conversion is weak and comparable listings offer a clearer value, improve the listing before cutting price.

## A practical pricing checklist

1. Calculate the complete order cost.
2. Confirm current Etsy fees for the seller country.
3. Model organic and Offsite Ads orders.
4. Test free and buyer-paid shipping.
5. Compare total checkout price with real competitors.
6. Recheck margin after the first 20 to 30 orders.

A sustainable Etsy price is transparent to the buyer and defensible in your books.
