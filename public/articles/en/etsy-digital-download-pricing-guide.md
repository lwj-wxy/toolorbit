# Etsy Digital Download Pricing: Price the File, Support, and License Together

Digital products have no shipping label, but they still require design time, testing, customer support, file maintenance, and clear licensing. A low price is not automatically competitive if the buyer cannot understand what is included.

Use Etsy's [digital item guidance](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015662587-How-to-List-a-Digital-Item) and check the current file and listing rules before publishing.

## Define the deliverable first

Before setting a price, write down:

- file formats and sizes;
- number of files or pages;
- editable or flattened status;
- personal or commercial use;
- software needed to open the files;
- included instructions and support.

The buyer should be able to understand the deliverable without guessing. A clear scope makes price comparisons more meaningful.

## Calculate the cost of a sale

Include the time spent designing, exporting, checking, documenting, updating, and answering questions. Add Etsy fees and a reasonable allowance for refunds or support. The cost per sale may be low, but the product still needs to recover the time required to create the first version and maintain it.

Use the [Etsy Pricing Calculator](/tools/ecommerce/etsy-pricing-calculator) for the platform fee scenario. Then decide how many sales are needed to recover the original design effort.

## Compare bundles carefully

A bundle can increase order value when the files solve related tasks. Group items around a clear use case instead of adding unrelated files just to make the package larger. Explain the individual contents and the saving compared with buying them separately.

For multiple license tiers, make the difference concrete. A personal-use file, small-business license, and extended commercial license should have clear permissions and limits.

## Price against value and alternatives

Competitor prices are useful context, not a rule. Compare the time the buyer saves, the quality of the file, the included formats, and the support provided. A niche template that removes a difficult setup step can be worth more than a generic file with the same number of pages.

## Reduce support before lowering price

Many digital-download problems come from unclear instructions. Include:

- a short start-here guide;
- a list of included files;
- software compatibility;
- printing or export notes;
- license terms;
- contact instructions for missing or corrupted files.

Improving the delivery experience can protect reviews and reduce support time without making the product cheaper.

## Digital product checklist

- The file scope is clear in the first paragraph.
- The preview images show the actual contents.
- The price covers design and support time.
- License permissions are written in plain language.
- The download instructions are tested by someone unfamiliar with the product.
- The listing complies with Etsy's current digital-item rules.

Price a digital product as a complete customer experience, not as an empty download link.
